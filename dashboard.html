    How the score works

    • Days / week (Gym, HIIT): done = 100. Skipped = 100 while the weekly target is still reachable, 0 once it isn't. Skips need a reason.
    • At most per day (Snacking, Scrolling, Procrastination): at or under = 100, falling to 0 at double. A target of 0 is strict: 1 over = 40, 2 = 10, 3+ = 0.
    • At least per day (Meaningful work): value ÷ target, capped at 100.
    • Per week totals (Job hunting, ad hoc): are you on pace? Running total ÷ the share of the target due by today.
    • Task timer: each session starts in procrastination mode; time until you press “Start working” counts as procrastination, the rest as work (paused time counts as neither; after Resume you're procrastinating again until you press Back to work). Each session's “% procrastinating” is procrastination ÷ (procrastination + work). Today’s totals pre-fill Meaningful work (hours and procrastination minutes) in the check-in, and you can edit them there.
    • Tasks (yes / no): 100 from the day it’s done; not counted while there’s still time; 0 if Friday ends without it.
    • Status on the board: Done when the weekly target is met; On track; At risk when there’s no slack left (or behind pace / under 80% of days on target); Missed when the target can’t be reached any more (or under 50% of days on target).
    • A goal is the average of its metrics; the day is the average of its goals. Green ≥ 67, amber 34–66, coral < 34.
